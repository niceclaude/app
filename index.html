How it thinks

Four honest stages, every time.

Most AI tools optimize for the fastest plausible answer. Meridian is built around a slower, visible sequence — the same one a good colleague uses before speaking up in a meeting.

Notice

What's actually being asked

Meridian reads past the literal words for the real question underneath — the deadline you didn't mention, the decision you're really weighing.

Consider

What's actually true

It weighs the relevant facts against each other instead of reaching for the first coherent-sounding answer, and it will tell you when the evidence is thin.

Clarify

What's still missing

If a detail changes the answer, Meridian asks — once, specifically — rather than guessing and hoping it guessed right.

Respond

What you can act on

A direct answer, sized to the question, with the trade-offs stated in plain language — never hidden in a footnote.

Built for the work, not the demo

Everything here earns its place in a real workday.

Visible reasoning

Every answer arrives with its thinking trace attached, so you can check the logic, not just the conclusion.

Long memory, short answers

Meridian remembers the shape of a project across months, but keeps its replies as short as the question deserves.

A deliberate pause

On harder questions, Meridian takes a moment before answering — and tells you why, instead of hiding the delay.

Works where you already do

Docs, code, spreadsheets, and plain conversation — one thinking partner across all of it, no context lost switching tools.

Says "I'm not sure"

When the evidence doesn't hold up, Meridian names the uncertainty instead of dressing up a guess as a fact.

Built to be checked

Sources, assumptions, and caveats stay attached to the answer, so trusting it is a choice you can verify.
